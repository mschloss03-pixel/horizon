How forecasts are graded

In-range rate: the share of forecasts where the real price ended up inside the 10–90% range. A well-calibrated model lands near 80%.

Skill vs simple baseline: 1 − pinball loss / baseline_hist pinball loss. Above 0 means the forecast beats a simple historical baseline; below 0 means it does not.

Live forecasts are graded after their target date passes. Backtest forecasts replay the model on past data (walk-forward, every 4th start date, train + validation periods).
